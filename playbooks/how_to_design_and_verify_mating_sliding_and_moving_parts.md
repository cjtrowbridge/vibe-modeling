# Playbook: Design and Verify Mating, Sliding, and Moving Parts

*Status: Stable*

## Objective

Verify lids, drawers, rails, hinges, gimbals, latches, and removable panels across
their complete installation and motion envelopes.

## Procedure

1. Define fixed datums, degrees of freedom, hard stops, retention features,
   insertion/removal path, and intended operating range.
2. Model mating components and swept envelopes from the same named parameters.
3. Apply tolerance-stack rules to guide clearance, end clearance, angular error,
   sag, fastener protrusion, and printer variation.
4. Inspect the start, midpoint, end, and worst rotated/translated states.
5. Verify rails, lips, pins, walls, and retention tabs for continuous attachment
   and minimum material after all clearance cuts.
6. Confirm assembly order and tool/hand access; a collision-free final position
   does not prove installability.
7. Check anti-rattle capture, binding risk, over-travel, cable routing, and service
   removal independently.
8. Use coupons or partial assemblies for uncertain friction and flex behavior.

## Verification

- Full swept and installation envelopes are collision-free at required clearance.
- Retention works without relying on unintended interference.
- Structural margins pass in both fixed and moving members.
- Physical motion claims have test evidence or remain unverified.

## Plan Binding

The plan must define motion states, clearance targets, retention behavior, and
assembly sequence acceptance criteria.

## Lifecycle Compliance

Prompt -> Select/Create Plan -> Request approval -> Execute approved plan items -> Plan update -> Docs/journal update -> Verification.
