# Rev 0001 Detailed Candidate

- State: `detailed_generic_receiver_candidate`
- Product: `cyberdeck_2_product`
- Subassembly: `split_2u_receiver_enclosure`
- Printable leaves: `enclosure_left`, `enclosure_right`
- Split: longitudinal product datum `X = 0`
- Front: open receiver bay with twelve insert positions
- Rear: closed by an integral 3 mm wall

This revision completes the requested generic enclosure geometry. The two leaves
retain strict shell ownership on opposite sides of the split. The former
top/bottom bumpouts have been removed: the complete 124.5 mm height is a flush
rectangular shell, with 17.8 mm protected service zones above and below the
222.25 x 88.90 mm device envelope. Continuous 3 mm fascia strips close the
front face outside the opening and structurally tie both insert rails into the
front frame.

At each of four stations, the left leaf owns a fully enclosed captive-nut
receiver socket and the right leaf owns a 3 mm tongue that enters only through
the center-seam mouth. The socket has 1 mm fit clearance on every non-insertion
face and its closed end, with 3 mm structural walls around the cavity. Top
screws install downward through recessed top holes; bottom screws install
upward through recessed bottom holes.
Two continuous 3 mm lower shelves overlap the generic device footprint by 3 mm
and transfer its weight into both side walls, the front rails, and the rear wall.

The candidate is intentionally mutable and is not published to `revisions/`.
Exact device and process inputs remain `BLOCKED_UNKNOWN`: body/face dimensions,
mass, center of gravity, connectors, cables, airflow, service access, insert
product, printer, material, slicer profile, finished-hole calibration, support
strategy, and physical fit.

## Angled Screen Interface Checkpoint

The closed `Y = 215 mm` wall is now the user-facing front. At the open `Y = 0 mm`
back end, the same two printable leaves carry a face-local 2U screen interface at
45 degrees toward the front. The interface has the rack-standard `254 mm` outside
width, a `222.25 x 88.90 mm` clear aperture, and two six-hole M3 blind-insert
columns. Its 50.8 mm maximum rear face-normal envelope projects 62.862 mm across
the face and 35.921 mm behind it, so the backmost upper point is exactly at
`Y = 0` and the structure stays within the original 215 mm product depth. The
assembled top becomes `Z = 125.112 mm`.

Only the left/right face rails and their 3 mm outer support walls are present;
the top, rear, and centre above the lower receiver are intentionally open. A
specific screen panel, its actual aperture, cable-bend space, mounting stack,
and any roof/lid remain `BLOCKED_UNKNOWN` until the physical hardware is supplied.
