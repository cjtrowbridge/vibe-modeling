# Micro Cyberdeck Fan Guard

Key: `[ ]` pending task, `[x]` completed task, `[?]` needs validation, `[-]` closed task

Context: the micro_cyberdeck_case right (fan) wall carries a 40 mm fan with a
Ø38 mm air opening and four Ø4.2 mm mount holes on a 32 mm pitch, centered at
the wall midpoint. The guard is a new independent design in `designs/fan_guard/`
that clamps to the fan's four corner holes through matching Ø4.2 mm studs.
